# Social Media Marketing Plan

An executable, low-production plan for **The Sporty Way (TSW)** covering
**19 August to 8 November 2026**. It assumes the social accounts are starting
properly now and does not rely on any existing league traction.

The operating principle is simple:

> TSW generates basketball content → choose the two strongest assets → publish
> them on Instagram and TikTok → learn what earns attention.

## Plan At A Glance

| Decision           | Plan                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Primary objective  | Discover which repeatable basketball formats earn attention and create player or organiser interest.                                     |
| Main audiences     | Players/basketball fans first; league organisers second.                                                                                 |
| Channels           | **Instagram and TikTok only** for the first 12 weeks.                                                                                    |
| Core cadence       | Two content ideas per week, adapted and posted to both platforms. That is two production tasks, not four.                                |
| Posting days       | Tuesday at 19:00 and Friday at 12:30, UK time, after the launch week. Treat these as a consistent test, not universal “best times”.      |
| Optional activity  | Repost a tag or core post to Instagram Stories. Do not create separate Story content to satisfy a quota.                                 |
| Weekly time budget | 60-75 minutes batching content, 30-40 minutes engaging, and 10 minutes reviewing results: **about two hours**.                           |
| Content balance    | Roughly 70% player/fan interest, 20% indirect organiser proof, 10% direct product explanation.                                           |
| Conversion         | Players: view a profile or join. Organisers: view a league demo or send an enquiry. Until public pricing is ready, do not use “buy now”. |
| Paid promotion     | None during this test. Organic performance should identify the creative worth amplifying first.                                          |

Do not add a third publishing platform during this period. WhatsApp or email can
be used for a small amount of one-to-one organiser follow-up, but neither needs a
separate content calendar. Reconsider a third channel only after week 12 and only
if the same assets can be reused with almost no additional work.

## Positioning And Audience

**One-line promise:** TSW turns a basketball game into stats, stories, profiles,
and highlights that players want to share.

| Audience                             | What they care about                                                                              | What the content should show                                                                                                                     | Useful CTA                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Players and basketball fans          | Recognition, comparison, memorable performances, highlights, and a record of progress             | A name and face, verified stat line, final score, shot map, milestone, leader list, or clip                                                      | “Would you want your games tracked like this?” / “See the full box score.” |
| League organisers and administrators | Professional presentation, player engagement, less duplicate work, and a better league experience | The output of an organised league: current table, public profiles, automatic recaps, shareable cards, and one update flowing through the product | “See what a game produces.” / “Message us to run a demo game.”             |

Players create the pull; organisers decide whether a league adopts the product.
Most posts should therefore be good basketball content first and product evidence
second. Avoid a feed of feature announcements.

Use British English: _fixtures_, _table_, _organiser_, and _kit_. Use a real
statistic only when it comes from TSW's recorded data. Demo data must be labelled
as a demo when a reasonable viewer could mistake it for a real competition.

## Five Recurring Content Pillars

Every calendar post belongs to one of these formats. The repeated structure is
intentional: the variable is the game, player, or question, not the design.

| Pillar                           | Repeatable format                                                                          | Main audience | Share of posts | Product source                                        | Typical manual work                                             |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------- | -------------: | ----------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1. Stat Receipt**              | Player game line, top performers, milestone, or player progress                            | Players/fans  |            35% | Box score, player profiles, milestones, season trends | 5-20 min until the social-kit backlog ships; 2-5 min afterwards |
| **2. Game Story**                | Final score, box-score breakdown, shot-map question, or Game of the Week                   | Players/fans  |            25% | Game recap, box score, shot map                       | 10-20 min; mostly selection and carousel ordering               |
| **3. Highlight + Context**       | A short play followed by the recorded stat/result                                          | Players/fans  |            10% | YouTube-backed event timestamps and game recap        | 15-25 min now; 3-5 min after templated clip export              |
| **4. League Looks Professional** | The public table, player profiles, fixtures, and recaps presented as one league experience | Organisers    |            20% | Public league pages and admin workflow                | 10-20 min using screenshots and one reusable carousel           |
| **5. Tap, Track, Done**          | A 6-12 second screen recording showing one satisfying product loop                         | Both          |            10% | Live tracker or fixture-to-recap workflow             | 10-15 min; no narration or on-camera filming                    |

If a pillar needs a new design every time, the format is broken. Simplify it or
stop using it.

## Social Asset Standard

### Export Formats

| Preset       | Output                     | Use                                                            |
| ------------ | -------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Feed/card    | 1080x1350 PNG, 4:5         | Instagram feed and carousel; reusable in a TikTok photo post   |
| Vertical     | 1080x1920 PNG or MP4, 9:16 | Instagram Stories/Reels, TikTok, and video covers              |
| Link preview | 1200x630 PNG, 1.91:1       | Public links shared in DMs, messaging, and other link surfaces |

Instagram accepts Reels up to 9:16 and recommends at least 720p/30fps; TikTok's
own creative guidance also prioritises 9:16 and keeping key information clear of
interface overlays. These are the output targets, not a reason to turn every
static statistic into a video.

### Visual Hierarchy

Every asset should answer these questions in order:

1. **Why stop?** A short headline: “42 POINTS”, “FINAL”, “TRIPLE-DOUBLE”, or
   “WHO WON THIS MATCH-UP?”
2. **Who and where?** Player name/photo, team identities, competition, and date.
3. **What happened?** Score or the three to five statistics that prove the hook.
4. **What next?** One CTA: “Full box score in profile”, “Save this”, “Who gets
   your vote?”, or “Run a demo game”.

Additional design rules:

- Make the score or achievement the largest object. Names come before secondary
  stats; branding comes last.
- Use tabular figures, high contrast, and type sized for a phone, not small labels
  copied from the product UI.
- Keep TSW's layout consistent while using team colours as accents. Always test
  contrast and long names.
- Use a real player portrait when permission exists. Otherwise use an explicit
  initials treatment; do not make a team logo look like the player's picture.
- Use a small stable `@TheSportyWay`/URL footer. Avoid a large watermark that
  competes with the basketball story.
- Generate descriptive alt text from the same verified fields as the card.

### Safe Areas

For a 1080x1920 asset, keep the hook, names, score, and CTA inside a conservative
central zone: leave at least 60 px on the left, 180 px on the right, 180 px at the
top, and 360 px at the bottom. This is an internal working guide, not a permanent
platform specification. Preview every vertical template in both apps because
captions, buttons, and profile-grid crops change.

The product should provide separate Instagram and TikTok safe-area overlays in
preview and never bake the guide into the export. A cover must still make sense
when cropped; keep the headline and subject near the centre rather than at an
edge.

### Motion Rules

Use motion for only three things: a real highlight, a screen interaction, or a
stat reveal. A reusable video needs no talking head and no timeline editing:

1. 0-2 seconds: headline/hook;
2. 2-7 seconds: clip, screen action, or two-stat reveal;
3. 7-10 seconds: score/context and one CTA.

Burn essential words into the video so it works muted. Optional audio should
come from the platform's licensed library when posting; do not make a particular
trending sound part of the template.

## Low-Friction Production System

### One-Time Setup In Week 1

- Use professional/creator accounts so insights are available. Use the same
  handle, profile image, one-line promise, and landing link on both platforms.
- Bio: **“Basketball stats, profiles and highlights — tracked live and ready to
  share. Built for players and leagues.”**
- Create one 4:5 carousel shell and one 9:16 screen-recording shell. Do not create
  a third design template during the 12-week test.
- Create a working folder with `01-ready`, `02-posted`, and `03-results`.
- Use the demo account for evergreen content. Use identifiable real people only
  with recorded permission; get parent/guardian permission before promotional
  use involving a minor.
- Add source-specific landing links when attribution exists:
  `?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=organic_social&utm_campaign=launch_2026q3`
  and the TikTok equivalent.

### Caption Formula

Use five short components:

1. **Hook:** the exact search-friendly subject, result, or question.
2. **Proof:** one or two verified facts from the card.
3. **Conversation:** a genuine basketball question.
4. **CTA:** one action only.
5. **Discovery:** 3-5 specific hashtags after the copy.

Example:

> London basketball player stat line: 28 PTS, 9 REB, 6 AST. The fourth-quarter
> run made the difference. What stands out most — the scoring or the all-round
> line? Save the box score. #LondonBasketball #UKBasketball

For TikTok, put the exact subject phrase in the on-screen headline and first
caption line, such as “London basketball player stats” or “How to track a
basketball game”. TikTok says search matching, hashtags, sound, location, and
viewer behaviour can inform discovery, with content match weighted strongly in
search. Check Creator Search Insights for five minutes each Sunday; do not chase
an unrelated trend.

## Discoverability Without Hashtag Spam

### Captions And Keywords

- Put the searchable subject in normal language in the first line: “London
  basketball stats”, “UK basketball league table”, “basketball shot chart”, or
  “how to track a basketball game”.
- Put the same phrase in the first frame/on-image headline when it reads
  naturally. Do not repeat awkward keyword variations.
- Lead with the basketball story, not “TSW feature update”. Product terms belong
  in the proof or CTA.
- Use one genuine question. Avoid “comment YES” or unrelated engagement bait.

### Hashtag Sets

Use **3-5** per post: one location, one or two community/competition terms, one
content term, and optionally the brand. Check that each is active and relevant in
the app before adding it.

| Post                     | Example set                                                                                                         |
| ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| London game/player       | `#LondonBasketball #UKBasketball #GrassrootsBasketball #BasketballStats`                                            |
| Analysis                 | `#UKBasketball #BasketballAnalysis #ShotChart #BasketballStats`                                                     |
| Organiser                | `#UKBasketball #GrassrootsBasketball #SportsTechnology #LeagueManagement`                                           |
| University/local variant | Replace the broad location with the real borough, city, university, or competition tag; do not stack all locations. |

`#TheSportyWay` can catalogue brand posts but should not be expected to generate
discovery. Do not use generic blocks such as `#fyp #viral #explorepage` unless the
term genuinely describes the post, which it usually does not.

### Tags, Locations, And Collaborations

- Tag a player, team, league, videographer, or venue only when featured and only
  when the handle is verified. Ask permission before using an Instagram Collab.
- Add the real venue/borough location when it helps local discovery. Never add a
  false central-London location to appear more local.
- Use an Instagram Collab for the strongest consented player/team post, at most
  once a week. An accepted Collab can appear on both profiles and reach both
  audiences. Current Instagram help says Collabs are not supported in scheduled
  posts, so publish those manually and schedule everything else.
- Credit source footage in the asset/caption and confirm the right to repost it.

### TikTok Search Check

Every Sunday, spend five minutes in Creator Search Insights:

1. search a core topic such as “UK basketball”, “London basketball”, “basketball
   stats”, or “basketball league”;
2. note a relevant query or content gap;
3. use it only if one of the two planned assets answers it;
4. after posting, check search analytics and record the query that surfaced the
   post.

## Measurement

### Scorecard

Record each post after seven complete days. Keep Instagram and TikTok rows
separate even when the creative is the same.

| Field                                         | Why it matters                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Date, platform, pillar, format, subject, hook | Identifies what was actually tested                                               |
| Views and accounts reached                    | Distribution; views may include replays, reach is unique accounts where available |
| Average watch time/completion for video       | Whether the first frame and pacing held attention                                 |
| Shares, saves, and comments                   | Stronger evidence of usefulness than likes alone                                  |
| Profile visits and follows from post          | Whether the content created curiosity about TSW                                   |
| Bio/landing visits by UTM                     | Whether curiosity moved off-platform                                              |
| DMs and qualified league enquiries            | Whether the right people raised a hand                                            |
| Player registrations and activation           | Whether visitors created an account and did something meaningful                  |
| Production minutes                            | Whether the format is sustainable                                                 |

A **qualified league enquiry** is from an organiser/decision-maker and includes a
real competition or concrete follow-up. A generic emoji DM is engagement, not an
enquiry. A **player activation** should be defined in analytics (for example,
profile claimed or first team/game action), not merely an email submitted.

### Working Targets

These are learning targets for an account starting from a small or zero audience,
not forecasts. Combined views mean the sum of 16 monthly platform uploads: eight
content pieces published to two platforms. If the starting audience is larger,
replace the absolute view number with a target of 1.5x the month-one baseline.

| Metric                       |                    Month 1, weeks 1-4 |   Month 2, weeks 5-8 |  Month 3, weeks 9-12 |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------: | -------------------: | -------------------: |
| Content pieces shipped       |                                     8 |                    8 |                    8 |
| Combined IG + TikTok views   |                                2,000+ |               4,000+ |               6,000+ |
| Profile visits               |                                   40+ |                  75+ |                 110+ |
| Shares + saves               |                                   20+ |                  35+ |                  50+ |
| Meaningful comments + DMs    |                                    6+ |                  10+ |                  15+ |
| Measured website visits      |                                   20+ |                  35+ |                  50+ |
| Qualified league enquiries   |                                    1+ |                   2+ |                   3+ |
| Player registrations         |                                    3+ |                   5+ |                   8+ |
| Follower growth (secondary)  |                                   25+ |                  40+ |                  60+ |
| Average total operating time | At most 150 min/week while setting up | At most 120 min/week | At most 105 min/week |

If UTM capture is not implemented, record website traffic as “unknown”, not
zero, and prioritise the attribution backlog. Do not use follower growth to hide
the absence of shares, profile visits, or enquiries.

### Decisions At Weeks 4, 8, And 12

- Rank pillars separately on **attention** (views/watch), **value** (shares/saves),
  and **intent** (profile visits/DMs/enquiries). Do not collapse everything into
  one vanity score.
- Repeat a format when its median share/save rate or profile-visit rate is above
  the account median across at least three posts.
- Change the first frame/hook when reach is adequate but watch time or carousel
  continuation is weak.
- Change the CTA/landing page when profile visits are healthy but measured visits,
  signups, and DMs are weak.
- Keep low-reach organiser posts if they create qualified conversations. Judge
  them on intent, not player-content view counts.
- Simplify or drop any format that takes more than 25 manual minutes twice unless
  it directly creates a qualified enquiry.
- At week 12, keep the best two player/fan formats and the best organiser format.
  Those become the next quarter's default calendar and the priority for product
  automation.
